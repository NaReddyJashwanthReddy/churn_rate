Finance Team

Goal:

Understand money flow, customer value, cost structure, and what “churn impact” actually means in dollars.

Q1. What financial metrics matter most for this business?

Finance Answer:

We track:

Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
Churn rate (obviously)
Retention ROI
Q2. How much revenue does each customer generate?

Finance Answer:

On average:

Basic user → $10/month → ~$120/year
Premium user → $20/month → ~$240/year
Family user → $30/month → ~$360/year

But actual CLV depends on retention duration.

Q3. What is customer acquisition cost (CAC)?

Finance Answer:

Currently:

CAC = $120 per customer

This has increased recently due to competition.

Q4. What happens financially when a customer churns?

Finance Answer:

We lose future revenue immediately.

Example:

Premium user churns after 3 months:
→ We only earned $60
→ We lose remaining expected ~$180 CLV

So churn is not just loss of subscription — it's loss of future cash flow.

Q5. Do we currently measure churn impact in dollars?

Finance Answer:

Not properly.

We track churn percentage, but not:

Revenue lost per churn segment
Profit impact per campaign
ROI of retention actions
Q6. What is the cost of retention campaigns?

Finance Answer:

Approximate budget:

$300,000 per month total retention budget

Breakdown:

Discounts
Coupons
Email tools
Push notification systems
Q7. What is the concern with current retention strategy?

Finance Answer:

We are overspending.

We give discounts to users who would not have churned anyway.

So we are:

Losing money on unnecessary incentives
Not maximizing ROI
Not targeting high-value users properly
Q8. What would make finance happy?

Finance Answer:

If we can:

Reduce churn in high-value users (especially Premium)
Improve retention ROI (money saved > money spent)
Target only users likely to churn
Avoid unnecessary discounts
Q9. How do you want to evaluate a churn model?

Finance Answer:

Not accuracy.

We care about:

Revenue saved
Profit gained
Retention ROI
Cost per retained user
Q10. What is a “good” business outcome?

Finance Answer:

Example target:

Reduce churn by 1.5%
Save $500K+ monthly revenue
Maintain retention cost under budget
Achieve 2x–4x ROI on interventions
