First stakeholder: Business Stakeholder / CEO / Product Owner (person who owns the business problem)

Goal:

Understand business pain + KPIs + constraints.

Simulation:

Q1. What business problem are we solving?

Stakeholder Answer:

Customer churn increased from 6% to 8% over the last 4 months. Revenue growth slowed down.

Q2. Why is this important now?

Stakeholder Answer:

Acquiring new customers costs more than retaining existing ones. Higher churn directly reduces recurring revenue.

Q3. What does churn mean for this company?

Stakeholder Answer:

A customer is considered churned if they cancel their subscription or remain inactive for more than 30 days.

Q4. Which customer segment is most concerning?

Stakeholder Answer:

Premium users. Their churn increased significantly and they contribute the highest revenue.

Q5. What business KPI should improve?

Stakeholder Answer:

Reduce monthly churn from 8% → 6.5%.

Secondary KPIs:

Retention rate
Monthly recurring revenue
Customer lifetime value
Q6. What actions can business take if we identify churn risk?

Stakeholder Answer:

We can provide:

Discounts
Personalized recommendations
Retention emails
Loyalty rewards
Q7. Is there a budget constraint?

Stakeholder Answer:

Yes. Retention campaign budget is $300,000/month.

Q8. What business changes happened recently?

Stakeholder Answer:

Premium subscription price increased 2 months ago.

Q9. How do you currently identify churn risk?

Stakeholder Answer:

Simple rules:

No login for 14 days
Failed payments
Reduced activity

No ML system exists.

Q10. How will success be measured?

Stakeholder Answer:

Lower churn + positive ROI from retention actions.
