Stakeholder 2: Marketing Team (Retention + Growth + Campaigns)

Goal:

Understand how users are targeted, what campaigns exist, and how marketing influences churn.

Q1. How do you currently try to reduce churn?

Marketing Answer:

We run retention campaigns using:

Email offers
Push notifications
Discount coupons
Personalized content recommendations

Triggered mostly when users become inactive.

Q2. How do you define an “at-risk” user for campaigns?

Marketing Answer:

Simple rule-based system:

No login in last 14 days
Subscription renewal within 7 days
Low engagement detected manually in dashboards

No machine learning model is used.

Q3. What types of campaigns are running currently?

Marketing Answer:

3 main types:

Win-back campaigns (inactive users)
Upsell campaigns (Basic → Premium)
Engagement campaigns (increase usage frequency)
Q4. How are campaigns delivered?

Marketing Answer:

Through:

Email system
Mobile push notifications
In-app banners

All triggered via marketing automation tool.

Q5. Do you track campaign performance?

Marketing Answer:

Yes, but only basic metrics:

Open rate
Click-through rate (CTR)
Redemption rate for discounts

We don’t track long-term churn impact properly.

Q6. Recent campaign performance trend?

Marketing Answer:

Performance is declining:

Email open rate: 28% → 19%
Push CTR: 4% → 3.2%
Discount usage is stable (~12%) but not improving retention
Q7. What segment responds best to campaigns?

Marketing Answer:

New users respond well to onboarding emails
Premium users respond poorly to discounts
Basic users are most price sensitive
Q8. Are campaigns personalized or generic?

Marketing Answer:

Mostly generic.

Some personalization exists (like name + watch history), but no predictive targeting.

Q9. What is the biggest marketing challenge right now?

Marketing Answer:

We are targeting too many users blindly.

We send discounts even to users who would not have churned.

This is increasing cost without improving retention much.

Q10. What data do you wish you had?

Marketing Answer:

We want to know:

Who is actually going to churn (not just inactive users)
Which campaign will work for which user
Expected ROI before sending offers
