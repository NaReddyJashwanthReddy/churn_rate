Customer Support Team

Goal:

Understand customer complaints, friction points, and signals that precede churn.

Q1. What are the most common customer complaints?

Support Answer:

Recently, we see 4 major complaint categories:

Premium price increase complaints
App bugs after recent update
Poor recommendation quality
Payment failures / billing issues
Q2. Have complaints changed in the last few months?

Support Answer:

Yes, noticeably.

“Content not relevant” complaints increased
“App feels slower after update” increased
Billing complaints remained stable
Premium pricing complaints increased sharply after price change
Q3. Do churned users contact support more?

Support Answer:

Yes, strongly.

Average tickets per user:

Churned users → ~3 to 5 tickets
Active users → ~0 to 1 ticket

Most churned users contacted support before leaving.

Q4. When do users usually contact support before churn?

Support Answer:

Typically 1–3 weeks before cancellation.

Pattern we see:

Complaint or issue raised
No resolution or repeated frustration
Reduced usage
Cancellation
Q5. What are the most common unresolved issues?

Support Answer:

Recommendation not improving after feedback
Login issues on mobile app
Payment retry failures
Confusing UI after redesign
Q6. Are support tickets categorized?

Support Answer:

Yes, but manually:

Billing issues
Technical issues
Content issues
Account issues

But categorization is inconsistent sometimes.

Q7. Which users are most likely to complain?

Support Answer:

Premium users complain more (higher expectations)
Users after app update spikes in complaints
Users with failed payments are very high risk
Q8. Do we track sentiment or urgency?

Support Answer:

Not systematically.

Agents write notes, but we don’t have structured sentiment scoring.

Q9. What happens after a complaint is resolved?

Support Answer:

No formal tracking of outcome impact.

We don’t measure:

Did user stay?
Did churn reduce?
Did engagement improve?
Q10. What patterns do experienced agents notice before churn?

Support Answer:

Usually:

Increasing frustration tone in messages
Repeated similar complaints
“This used to be better” type feedback
Reduced response to support follow-ups
